# 03: Publish editable Training Services

**What to build:** Deliver the full Training Service path from validated editable content through Decap to the public Services route. The Editor must be able to maintain an ordered set of Training Services, while visitors receive restrained and accurate placeholder information.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Deliver the branded content-managed site shell.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Training Services form an ordered, validated collection exposed through Decap.
- [ ] Initial entries are Management System Design and Implementation, Competency Assessment and Development, and Leadership Coaching in that order.
- [ ] The Services route renders editable introductory Page Content followed by each Training Service’s name, summary, and restrained description.
- [ ] Adding, editing, reordering, or removing a Training Service changes the next production build without requiring a layout-code change.
- [ ] Missing required Training Service fields fail validation with actionable errors.
- [ ] Browser coverage verifies the initial order and content through the production build.
- [ ] No Training Service claims unsupported outcomes, accreditation, statistics, clients, or certifications.
