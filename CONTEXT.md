# KMSC Website

This context describes the public website and publishing language for KMSC.

## Language

**KMSC**:
Kautilya Management System Consultancy Pvt. Ltd., the consultancy business represented by this website.
_Avoid_: KMCS, Kautilya Management Consultancy Service

**Client Company**:
A company seeking professional training from KMSC.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, learner

**Training Service**:
A professional training offering marketed by KMSC to Client Companies.
_Avoid_: Training option, product, course

**Training Enquiry**:
A privacy-consented message from a Client Company describing its training needs.
_Avoid_: Lead, contact submission, support request

**Editor**:
The single authorized person who updates Page Content and creates, updates, and directly publishes Blog Posts.
_Avoid_: Author, administrator, contributor

**Page Content**:
The editable business copy and approved imagery shown on the Home, About, Services, and Contact pages.
_Avoid_: Blog Post, layout, source code

**Blog Post**:
An article published in the website's Blog and subsequently editable by the Editor.
_Avoid_: News item, page, entry

**Media Asset**:
An approved image associated with Page Content or a Blog Post.
_Avoid_: Arbitrary file, attachment
